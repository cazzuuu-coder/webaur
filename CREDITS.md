### Illustrative “before” processing

`rolls-before` and `front-before` are not separate photographs. They are derived from `rolls` and `front` with the same crop and framing, in this order:

1. Contrast reduced to about 70% and colour saturation to about 72%; brightness slightly lowered.
2. A warm-grey fill multiplied over the image to suggest a tired clearcoat.
3. A light Gaussian blur blended in so reflections lose their edge.
4. Fine monochrome noise at low opacity.
5. A final, slight flattening of contrast, with sharpness reduced.

The Results page states this clearly. The sliders are not presented as documentary before-and-after records of real work.
