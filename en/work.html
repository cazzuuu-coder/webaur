01 Concept project Barber

Hale Barber Studio

A five-page concept for an independent barbershop. The site is built around a quiet hour in the chair: bone, forest green, and photographs of the room, not stock smiles.

Design direction

Editorial and calm, and no longer in the studio’s own display face. Cormorant Garamond for the voice, Outfit for body text, a bone surface, forest green and a brass monogram.

Main features

  • Five pages: home, services, barbers, gallery, visit
  • A service menu with example prices
  • A staff introduction and a close-in gallery of the room
  • A visit page that does not pretend to take real appointments

Design decisions

  • A typographic wordmark instead of a drawn logo
  • Photographs of the room and the work, not lifestyle poses
  • A booking path that stays honest as a concept

02 Concept project Automotive detailing

Alto Detail

A dark showroom concept for a detailing studio: graphite, oxide red, and a cinematic opening with the car and the headline in the same frame. Five pages, including a results page whose sliders are the main object.

Key feature. The same car, twice. The sliders compare two treatments of the same photograph. They are illustrative, not evidence of paid work.

Design direction

Cinematic and industrial. Big Shoulders for an automotive voice, Source Sans 3 for reading, a graphite ground and an oxide-red accent — closer to a night bay than to a light-grey app.

Main features

  • Five pages: home, services, results, process, contact
  • Three named packages with example prices
  • Working before-and-after sliders, described honestly
  • A process page that walks through a day in the bay

Design decisions

  • A typographic mark that reads like a manufacturer’s name
  • Comparisons that admit the “before” frame is processed
  • Prices on the page, not behind a quote form

03 Concept project Café

Maison Café

A five-page café concept on parchment, bottle green and copper. The site is built like a short publication: a table, a menu with food photography, a room study, a story, and a visit page that admits it lives only in the story.

Menu

The room

Design direction

Warm and typographic. Bodoni Moda for the display voice, Manrope for body text, a parchment surface, bottle-green bands and a copper accent.

Main features

  • Five pages: home, menu, the room, story, visit
  • An editorial menu with example prices
  • A longer story page about the house
  • A visit page that does not invent a real door on a real street

Design decisions

  • A strong serif as the café’s voice
  • Food photographs in sequences, not a tiled catalogue
  • Hospitable writing that stays fictional and labelled
