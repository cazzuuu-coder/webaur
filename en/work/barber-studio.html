Work / Hale Barber Studio

Concept project

Hale Barber Studio

A five-page concept for an independent barbershop. The site is built around a quiet hour in the chair: bone, forest green, and photographs of the room, not stock smiles.

View live demo Back to work

Starting point

To design a complete barbershop site that would stand as commissioned work: its own identity, several real pages, and a visit experience without invented bookings or reviews.

Aim

To show how WEBAUR would approach a small service business: typography, photography and a clear path, without a generic template.

Audience

An independent barber who wants a site as careful as the cut — small, precise and calm, not loud advertising.

This is a concept project. It is not a paying client’s website, and it does not invent customers, reviews or results.

Main features

  • Five pages: home, services, barbers, gallery, visit
  • A service menu with example prices
  • A staff introduction and a close-in gallery of the room
  • A visit page that does not pretend to take real appointments

The visit page includes hours, a fictional address, and a booking form that does not send. It states plainly that this is a concept.

Design decisions

Let typography carry the brand.

Cormorant Garamond is the studio’s voice; Outfit keeps the body text readable. A brass monogram and typographic wordmark keep Hale distinct from WEBAUR’s own Instrument Serif.

Photograph the room, not a campaign.

The gallery and hero speak of chairs, light and tools. That is closer to a real trade than posed portraits.

Stay honest about contact.

A real business would need a real booking tool. This concept does not pretend that one already exists.
