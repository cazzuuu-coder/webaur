Work / Alto Detail

Concept project

Alto Detail

A dark showroom concept for a detailing studio: graphite, oxide red, and a cinematic opening with the car and the headline in the same frame. Five pages, including a results page whose sliders are the main object.

View live demo Back to work

Starting point

To build a complete detailing site with its own brand, priced packages, a visual comparison and a contact path that does not invent results.

Aim

To show how WEBAUR would treat a technical service brand: photography, typography and a function (the slider) that actually does something.

Audience

A detailing studio that wants to look as precise as the work — closer to a showroom than to loud garage advertising.

This is a concept project. It is not a paying client’s website, and it does not invent customers, reviews or results.

Main features

  • Five pages: home, services, results, process, contact
  • Three named packages with example prices
  • Working before-and-after sliders, described honestly
  • A process page that walks through a day in the bay

The sliders compare two treatments of the same photograph. They are illustrative, not evidence of paid work.

Design decisions

Give the brand a manufacturer’s voice.

Big Shoulders is wide and mechanical; Source Sans 3 keeps packages and process text readable. Oxide red is used sparingly, like a painted caliper.

Keep the comparison honest.

A slider is only convincing if the caption is true. Both sides are the same photograph; the “before” is a processed frame, and the page says so.

Put the price next to the package.

Essential, Signature and Atelier are listed with example figures so a visitor can judge the offer without a gate.
