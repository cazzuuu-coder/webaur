Legal

Privacy Policy

This is a basic policy structure. Legal entity details will be added once they are confirmed. No public email address is published on this site until one is set in js/site-config.js.

Data controller

The data controller is WEBAUR. The legal name, business ID and postal address will be published here when they are confirmed.

Contact

Enquiries: .

Until a contact email is configured, enquiries can only be prepared through the form on the contact page, and only once a sending method has been connected.

What we collect

If you submit the contact form we collect the name, business name, email, optional existing website, project type, optional budget and message you provide, plus a consent record. A honeypot field is used only to reduce spam and should be left empty.

Why we collect it

To respond to project enquiries. We do not use the form data for marketing lists unless you have separately agreed.

Processors

GitHub Pages does not process form submissions. If a Formspree form id is set in js/site-config.js, Formspree processes the submission. If only a contact email is configured, the form opens a message in your own email app and no third-party form processor is used. Analytics: none configured.

Retention and rights

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation and any resulting work. You may ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to the contact address once it is published. You may also contact a data protection authority in your country (in Finland, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman).
