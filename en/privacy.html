Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how WEBAUR handles the information you share when you contact us. WEBAUR has not yet published its official company details (legal name, business ID and postal address); they will be added here once they are confirmed.

Data controller and contact

WEBAUR, an independent web design studio based in Finland. Questions and requests about your data: hello@webaur.com.

What we collect

The contact form does not store or send anything by itself. It prepares an email in your own email app, and you decide whether to send it. If you do, we receive what you wrote: your name, business name, email address, the type of website, an optional package choice, an optional current website, your message and your consent. If you email us directly, we receive the content of that email.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry and, if you wish, to prepare a quote. The legal basis is your consent and steps taken at your request before a possible agreement. We do not add enquiries to marketing lists.

Where it is processed

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may log technical data such as visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Emails are handled in the email service WEBAUR uses. The site uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, and its fonts and images are served from the site itself.

Retention and your rights

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation and any resulting work. You may ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to hello@webaur.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in Finland, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.
