Frequently asked questions

What does each package include?

Each package lists the pages and features that are included. If you need something that is not on the list, we agree it separately before work begins.

Are the domain and hosting included?

No. Domain registration and website hosting are not part of the design and build packages. We can recommend straightforward, reliable options.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope and how smoothly drafts can be reviewed together. The timetable is agreed after the first conversation, once the work is clear — we do not promise a standard delivery date in advance.

Is ongoing maintenance included?

No. Maintenance is a separate service. The build packages cover the agreed design, development and launch of the website.

Can I use my own photographs and copy?

Yes. If you have photographs or text you want to use, we work with them. Photography or copywriting beyond the agreed scope is priced separately.

How many revision rounds are included?

Revision rounds are agreed in the quote, once the scope of the work is clear.
