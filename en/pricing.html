The website is yours

One payment, no commitment. With the finished site you get:

  • The finished, published website
  • The source code and files
  • Instructions for publishing and moving the site
  • The domain in your own name
  • The freedom to keep using the site without WEBAUR
  • The freedom to move the site to another provider or developer

Domain and hosting are not included in the package price. You pay them directly to the provider, and where possible they are registered in your own name and accounts.

Changes after launch

The site works without us. If you later want to change text, photos or prices, or add a service or a new page, we do it for a separately agreed one-time fee. The price is agreed before the work starts.

Before launch

  • We go through the content and the look of the site with you before launch.
  • Reasonable changes to text, images, colours and layout within the agreed scope are included.
  • Bugs found in the build are fixed at no extra cost.
  • New features, large redesigns and work outside the agreed scope are agreed separately.

Not included in the package price

  • Yearly domain fee
  • Hosting or web hotel fees
  • An online store (small from €1,900, larger by quote)
  • Extensive booking systems
  • Other special features agreed separately
  • New change work after launch

Please note

  • Content, price and timetable are confirmed in a written quote before work begins.

Frequently asked questions

Is Starter really one page?

Yes. Starter is one long page with several sections: introduction, services, prices and contact. The menu jumps straight to each section.

Does “up to 5 pages” mean five websites?

No. Business is one website with up to five separate pages, for example home, about, services, gallery and contact. Premium can have up to eight.

Are there monthly fees?

No. You pay WEBAUR once. The only recurring costs are the domain and hosting, plus the platform’s monthly fee for an online store, which you pay directly to the provider.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope and how smoothly drafts can be reviewed together. The timetable is agreed after the first messages, once the work is clear — we do not promise a standard delivery date in advance.

Can I use my own photographs and copy?

Yes. If you have photographs or text you want to use, we work with them. Photography or copywriting beyond the agreed scope is priced separately.

Do you build online stores or booking systems?

Yes, we build an online store if you want one. A small store (for example under about 30 products on a ready platform such as Shopify) starts from €1,900. You pay the platform’s monthly fee yourself, directly to the platform. Larger stores with hundreds of products, integrations, inventory management or several payment and shipping setups are priced by quote.

Scope and price are always confirmed in a written quote, and you pay WEBAUR once. Extensive booking systems and other special features are agreed separately in the same way.
