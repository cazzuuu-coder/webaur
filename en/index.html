The process

From the first conversation to a finished website you own.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We start by understanding the business, its customers and what the new site needs to do.

  2. 02

    Design

    We shape the visual direction, typography and page structure before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Development

    We build a responsive, carefully coded website that holds up on a phone and a desktop.

  4. 04

    Review and launch

    We go through the content and look with you, test in common browsers, fix any bugs and publish the site.

  5. 05

    Handover

    You receive the source code, the files and instructions for publishing and moving the site. It is yours to keep.
