The process

From the first conversation to launch — and after, if you want it.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We start by understanding the business, its customers and what the new site needs to do.

  2. 02

    Design

    We shape the visual direction, typography and page structure before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Development

    We build a responsive, carefully coded website that holds up on a phone and a desktop.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We test, publish and hand over the finished site, with a clear picture of what happens next.

  5. 05

    Support

    Optional ongoing maintenance: updates, small content changes and a named contact when something needs attention.
