03

How it works

Three steps, no surprises on the bill.

  1. Book or drop by

    Send the form below or bring the bike in. Tell us what feels wrong.

  2. We check and call

    A mechanic looks the bike over and tells you the price before starting.

  3. Ride away

    Pick it up cleaned and adjusted, with a short note of what was done.

04

A small workshop
that likes bikes.

Kaari is a two-stand workshop on a side street in Kallio. We work on one bike at a time, explain what we find in plain words, and never sell parts you don’t need.

Bring anything from a rusty city bike to a carbon road bike. If we can’t fix it well, we’ll say so.
