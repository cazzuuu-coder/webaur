Seasons

Two seasons.
Two kinds of light.

November – April

Snow from October to May, polar night around Christmas and the northern lights on clear nights. Days are short and blue, evenings are for the sauna and the sky.

  • Aurora on clear, dark nights
  • Husky, snowshoe and ski trails
  • Temperatures often well below zero

June – August

For about two months the sun doesn’t set. Lakes are still, the forest smells of pine and you can paddle at midnight in full daylight.

  • Midnight sun from late May
  • Paddling, hiking and lake swims
  • Berries and autumn colours from August
