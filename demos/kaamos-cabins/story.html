Our story

Built for
the long night.

Kaamos started with one cabin and one question: what would it feel like to really stop for a few days?

We wanted a place where the darkest time of the year feels like a gift, not something to get through.

So we kept things small. Three cabins, far enough apart that you only see your own lights. Big windows to the north. A sauna in each, because in Lapland that isn’t a luxury.

The cabins are built from local timber and painted black so they almost disappear into the forest. Inside it’s warm wood, wool and linen, and a stove that crackles all evening.

What we care about

Small on purpose.

  1. Space

    Three cabins only, so the forest stays quiet and the sky stays dark.

  2. Local

    Guides, food and firewood from people who live around Inari.

  3. Respect

    For the land, the reindeer herders’ routes and the Sámi culture of the area.

Getting here

Closer than

By car
Winter tyres required. The last kilometre is a ploughed forest road.
Arriving
