The barber

Aino Vartiainen.

A fictional name, written for this concept. The photographs are licensed stock, used to show how a small studio might introduce the person who holds the chair.

No years in the trade are claimed here, and no client reviews are invented. The page is about presence in the room, not a roster of awards.

The chair

Cuts, finishes, and a preference for unhurried work.

Aino is written as the studio’s only barber — short hair, longer hair, and the quieter work of a finish. One sitting at a time, against the times on the menu.

  • The cutShape first, then the line, then the finish. Written for short hair and for longer hair alike.
  • The shaveHot towels and a straight razor, only when the sitting has time enough not to rush the face.
  • The roomA small set of chairs, tools on the bench, and no queue at the door.
