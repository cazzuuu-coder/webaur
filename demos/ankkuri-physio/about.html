About us

A small clinic with time for you.

Ankkuri means anchor in Finnish. We opened the clinic to be exactly that: a steady, unhurried place where you understand what is going on with your body.

How we work

Three things we never skip.

  • Time to listenVisits are long enough to talk properly, not just treat and rush.

  • Exercise you can doShort home programmes that fit around work and family.
