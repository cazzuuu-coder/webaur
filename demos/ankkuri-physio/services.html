What we can help with.

Every visit starts by understanding the problem. Below you’ll find what each service involves, who it suits and what it costs.

01

Physiotherapy

Assessment and treatment for back, neck, shoulder, hip and knee pain, whether it started suddenly or has been there for years.

Good for
Aches and pain, stiffness, posture-related strain
What happens
Interview, movement tests, hands-on treatment and a home programme
Length
45 or 60 min

02

Sports injuries

A clear path back to running, the gym or the pitch, with loading that grows at the right pace.

Good for
Sprains, strains, overuse injuries, returning after a break
What happens
Assessment, a staged plan and regular check-ins as you progress
Length
45 min

03

Rehabilitation after surgery

Guided recovery after knee, hip or shoulder operations, in step with your surgeon’s instructions.

Good for
Recovery after joint replacement, ligament repair or arthroscopy
What happens
Mobility, strength and everyday movement, adjusted visit by visit
Length
45 or 60 min

04

Sports massage

Firm, focused massage for tight muscles, recovery between training sessions and general tension.

Good for
Active people, desk workers, anyone with tight shoulders or legs
What happens
Deep tissue massage and stretching, focused where you need it
Length
30 or 60 min

05

Small-group rehab

Weekly classes of up to six people, led by a physiotherapist, in our rehab room.

Good for
Back care, strength after an injury, staying active with joint pain
What happens
A guided exercise session with individual adjustments
Length
50 min, weekly
