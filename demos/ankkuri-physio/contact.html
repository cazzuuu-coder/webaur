Good to know

Before your visit

Do I need a doctor’s referral?

No. You can book physiotherapy directly. If you have a referral or notes from your doctor, bring them along.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in. For knee or hip problems, shorts are helpful.

How do I cancel?

Call or email at least 24 hours before your visit and there is no charge.
