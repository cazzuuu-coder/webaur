Services

The work, itemised.

Three example packages for this concept. Starting prices are illustrative, shown without VAT, and would be confirmed against the car and the work required. Nothing on this page is an offer from a real workshop.

01 · Essential

A careful wash, done by hand.

From €189 example starting price, excl. VAT

For a car that is already in decent condition and needs a thorough, safe clean rather than correction. The time in the bay is still unhurried.

Included in this example

  • Hand wash and dry, two-bucket method
  • Wheels, arches and door shuts
  • Interior vacuum and dust
  • Glass, inside and out
  • Tyre dressing
Enquire about Essential

02 · Signature

Decontaminate, then polish.

From €349 example starting price, excl. VAT

The core day’s work in this concept: the wash, a one-stage machine polish, and an interior that has been properly gone through — not just vacuumed.

Included in this example

  • Everything in Essential
  • Clay and chemical decontamination
  • One-stage machine polish
  • Interior deep clean, including plastics
  • A spray sealant, as agreed
Enquire about Signature

03 · Atelier

Correction, then protection.

From €590 example starting price, excl. VAT

For paint that needs more than a single stage, and for a ceramic coating agreed in writing before the work begins. The time in the bay may run into a second day, depending on the car.

Included in this example

  • Everything in Signature
  • Multi-stage correction, to the paint
  • Ceramic coating, as agreed
  • Engine-bay clean
  • Fabric or leather treatment
Enquire about Atelier
